## Open questions

- Can the Nature Communications (Bell nonlocality / Bayesian games) framework be extended beyond binary choices and two players? Not obviously straightforward.

Neither is a fit for the institution-generation angle from [quantum_pd.mdx](quantum_pd.mdx), which relies on genuinely quantizing the strategy space/payoffs, not just replacing a trusted mediator.
- For the Entropy 28 negotiation-games paper: understand the specific games used, how they're embedded in a quantum framework, and whether the quantization protocol matches the pattern used in [quantum_pd.mdx](quantum_pd.mdx).
- ~~Does Grau-Climent et al. Still open: whether an entangled equilibrium avoids overexploitation the way quantum_pd's (Q,Q) avoids the classical defect equilibrium — need to work through §3.2/§5 stability + bifurcation results.
- Is the "LDM entanglement = classical deterministic clearing/tax rule" equivalence already published, or is it a genuine extension of van Enk-Pike to the CV case? Read arXiv:1802.00260 (defense of van Enk-Pike) and arXiv:1701.08573 (Hawk-Dove counterexample) to check — see Follow-up reading table.
- Does "maximally entangled ⇒ Nash equilibrium profits equal Pareto optimal" (surfaced from Grau-Climent's own follow-up work / the general-entanglement-operator Cournot paper) contradict the "no finite most-quantum γ" conclusion reached for entropy-25 in [quantum_cpr_v2.ipynb](../../notebooks/quantum_cpr_v2.ipynb)? Possibly these papers define "maximal entanglement" as a parameter regime/limit rather than a hard bound — needs checking directly.

## Key ideas / concepts

- **The LDM/CV entanglement operation has no discrete "flip" analogue** (unlike EWL's Q̂). The displacement group is abelian (all $\hat D_i(x)$ commute), so there's no non-commuting move to play the role $\sigma_x$/Q̂ plays in EWL; the continuous strategy space already contains whatever "better" uptake entanglement shifts the equilibrium toward, so entanglement bends the equilibrium rather than unlocking a new discrete option.
- **Working hypothesis, not yet verified against literature:** the LDM/CV entangling operation $\hat J(\gamma)$ reduces to a purely classical, _deterministic_ mechanism — a fixed linear "clearing rule" (a referee/smart contract applies $g_i^c = g_i\cosh\gamma + g_j\sinh\gamma$ to both players' declared uptakes before computing payoffs). This is a stronger and structurally different reduction than van Enk-Pike's classical EWL model: EWL's classical equivalent needs a _randomizing_ mediator (a correlated coin — Aumann-style correlated equilibrium), while LDM's needs no randomness at all, just a public deterministic spillover/tax coefficient $\gamma$. Institutionally this maps onto a Pigouvian tax or quota/cap-and-trade system rather than a lottery — closer to Ostrom's actual CPR-governance toolkit than EWL's mediator was. **Needs literature check** (see follow-up reading above) before treating as original.
