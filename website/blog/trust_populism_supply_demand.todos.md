# Critique: Warum sinkendes Vertrauen den Populismus nicht erklärt

**Target audience:** Curious German reader without stats training, but comfortable with a chart and a number — the same reader as `sprit_national.mdx` (real data tables in the body, methodology appendix at the end). Inferred from the plan file's audience section, which the post still matches in topic but no longer matches in depth.
**Plan file:** Found (`trust_populism_supply_demand.plan.md`). The post deviates from the approved plan substantially: the central model result (plan §2, including the levels-vs-changes split), the "half nobody measures" argument with its evidence (plan §5), the concrete "what would help" section (plan §6), and the entire Technische Details appendix are all gone. The slimmed post keeps only the plan's setup (§1) and literature (§3, shortened).
**Overall impression:** The slimming genuinely works for the middle — the literature section and the ESS/PopuList setup are tighter and better for it. But it amputated the two things that made this post different from a newspaper column: the quantitative negative result (never shown, only asserted) and the concrete supply-side payoff. The post now peaks at the end of "Wissenschaftliche Befunde" and then stalls: the last two sections retreat into hedges and end in a shrug. Where it hangs most: everything after the literature section.

## Critical Issues

- [ ] **[§ Angebot und Nachfrage] "keinerlei Methoden finden, die diese Angebotsseite auch quantifizieren" — the most vulnerable sentence in the post.** The claim is factually overstated: quantified supply measures exist (CHES anti-elite salience per party/election, the V-Dem V-Party populism index, PopBERT speech intensity, Niedermayer's membership series by Bundesland). None of them resolves region × time the way the argument needs — *that* is the defensible claim, and the previous draft made it ("kaum gemessen", "alles national"). Worse: the repo now contains `notebooks/candidate_density_de_nuts1.csv`, the author's own quantified regional supply measure built from Bundeswahlleiterin data. A reader who opens the notebooks — the audience this blog writes for — will find the post's central "nobody can measure this" claim refuted by the author's own work. Fix the absolute or the post loses trust exactly where it needs it.
- [ ] **[§ Das naheliegende Modell] The post announces a model and then never shows it.** The intro promises "ein Modell, das sauber trennt, was die Daten hergeben und was nicht", and the reader gets three paragraphs of visual inspection plus the assertion "es war mir auch quantitativ nicht möglich". No number, no interval, no statement of what the model actually did. The `sprit_national` calibration reader expects bolded results and an appendix; here the unique selling point — a model that separates the national trend from the within-region relationship — is claimed and then hidden. Either restore the plain-language core result (the "14 region-election pairs learned nothing; that is not-knowing, not a null effect" statement, ideally with one number and its interval) or rewrite the intro promise. Plan §2 delivered neither its finding nor the finance analogy.
- [ ] **[§ Angebot und Nachfrage → Zusammenfassung] The last third of the post has no payoff.** The section names the supply/demand framework, concedes it is a "schicke qualitative Theorie ohne Vorhersagekraft", lists two data sources, and ends with "Es bleibt aber unklar inwieweit dies erfolgreich wäre" — and the Zusammenfassung then trails off in fragments. The reader who stayed through the literature section gets neither the punchline (demand is measured exhaustively, supply is not — that asymmetry *is* the post) nor any concrete way out. The author's own analysis produced exactly the missing material: candidate coverage 0.53 → 0.97 between 2013 and 2017, saturating in the same election after which trust turns downward, with the East/West divide invisible to it. One or two sentences of that turn this ending from a shrug into the post's actual finding.
- [ ] **[§ Zusammenfassung] The closer is grammatically broken and intellectually vague.** "Wenn man also bei dem einen Fortschritte erzielt, vermutlich auch beim anderen. Wie bleibt unklar..." — two fragments, one of them syntactically incomplete, ending a 1,000-word analysis in "un klar...". This is where the reader closes the tab without a takeaway. The plan's spine ("honest about the negative result — a genuine finding, not a hedge") is inverted here.
- [ ] **[§ imports] `TrustVoteChart` is imported but never used.** Dead import; either the post needs the second chart (the East/West gap discussion references data the tuner alone does not show) or the import must go. Note `sprit_national` and `kuesten_dimension` both mint a `tokenID` — this post has none, fine pre-mint, but the publishing checklist will need it.

## Suggestions

- [ ] **[§ Wissenschaftliche Befunde] Gidron & Hall is an orphaned one-liner.** "kommen von der anderen Seite und argumentieren, dass sich die Wähler vor allem sozial marginalisiert führen" — the reader cannot tell why this study is here. Algan and Scheiring each get an explicit connection back to trust; Gidron & Hall gets none. One clause stating that social marginalization is *also* not trust would tie it in. (And "führen" must be "fühlen".)
- [ ] **[§ Das naheliegende Modell] The leap from "visual observation" to "quantitativ nicht möglich" is unbridged.** With the appendix gone, the reader must take the negative result purely on faith. A single sentence stating what the model actually checked — region by region, election by election, after removing the shared national election component — makes the claim checkable instead of devotional.
- [ ] **[§ Angebot und Nachfrage] The appeal to authority arrives unsupported.** "wird in der Politikwissenschaft mittlerweile davon ausgegangen" — the slimming cut Mudde and Van Kessel, so the framework's provenance is now an anonymous consensus. One citation (Van Kessel's 31-country synthesis is the most citable) or drop the authority claim and argue it directly.
- [ ] **[§ pacing] Three hedges in the load-bearing positions.** "Es scheint also recht klar...", "Interessanterweise, konnte ich...", "Insgesamt scheint die Beobachtung gerechtfertigt..." — a post whose finding is a null result must report that finding with confidence (per the plan's own tone rule). The hedges read as uncertainty about the author's own analysis, not honesty about the data.
- [ ] **[§ Angebot und Nachfrage] The freshest finding in the author's own material is absent.** The post states the trust turn (~2017) in section 1 and could state the supply build-out (2013–2017) in section 4 — but never puts them next to each other. The sequencing observation (supply completed its build-out, then trust broke; correlation in time, no direction established) is the honest macro-level takeaway this post's audience will remember. It exists in the notebooks and costs two sentences.
- [ ] **[§ Angebot und Nachfrage] The two data bullets end mid-thought.** "Die Unterlagen des Bundeswahlleiters enthalten die Direktkandidaten je Wahlkreis." and the membership bullet list *sources* but never say what one would *do* with them (measure coverage; measure organizational reach). The reader cannot evaluate "Es bleibt unklar inwieweit dies erfolgreich wäre" because the proposal was never stated precisely enough to succeed or fail.
